# Presenter guide — the 10-minute pitch

## Before you start

1. Open `index.html` (double-click). No internet needed.
2. Move the window to the projector display, press **F** for presentation mode.
3. Press **P** once to start the clock, then press **P** again to hide the panel if the audience
   can see your screen. The clock keeps running while the panel is closed.
4. Chapter 01 should be on screen — the phone, with order notifications already arriving —
   before you say anything.

If the venue machine is slow or the projector is dim, press **M** to drop the motion layer.
Nothing is lost — every number and diagram stays.

---

## The shape of the talk

**Seller → Complexity → Risk → TicTaxFlow → Magic Moment → The Real Product → Market → Business
Model → Why Us → Mission**

Chapters 01–04 build the problem. **Do not mention the product before Chapter 05.** The whole
pitch depends on the audience wanting a solution before they hear the name.

## How to open

Chapter 01 has **no text at all**. Let the phone run for three or four seconds in silence: the
audience will watch the notifications stack up and the "orders today" counter climb, and they
will get the point before you open your mouth. Then speak the opening script over it. The two
Thai lines that used to be printed on this slide — ขายดีขึ้นทุกวัน and
แต่คุณรู้ไหมว่า… ต้องเสียภาษีเท่าไร? — are now yours to say, and they land harder spoken.

## Three places to slow down

- **Chapter 01**, at the very start. Three to four seconds of the phone before your first word.
- **Chapter 03**, after the counter reaches ⚠ ฿1.80M. Say nothing for a beat. The frame flashes
  once; let it.
- **Chapter 05**, after the name appears. One to two full seconds of silence. This is the single
  most important pause in the pitch.
- **Chapter 07**, after the laptop settles. Stop talking and let the judges read the screen — it is
  the real product. Count to three, then point at the card: the number, the distance, the warning.

## Where to cut if you are running long

Cut *depth*, not chapters — the story breaks if a chapter disappears.

1. Chapter 04: read only two of the three Thai questions.
2. Chapter 09: give the band (฿200K–฿1.8M) and the reason, drop the funnel commentary.
3. Chapter 06: name the three assistants without explaining each.

If you are three minutes over at Chapter 09, skip straight to 10 (`0` then `1`… or press `1` `0`)
and keep 11 and 12 intact. Never cut Chapter 12.

## If a judge pushes

- **"How big is the market?"** — Say plainly: we have not fabricated a number. The targeting is
  sellers in the ฿200K–฿1.8M band; sizing needs third-party data and we would rather show you the
  method than a made-up figure. (This is also on the slide.)
- **"Are you integrated with Shopee/Lazada/LINE?"** — No. That is roadmap and it needs
  integrations that do not exist. Chapter 08 says so on screen, under the laptop.
- **"Is the ฿1.8M threshold right?"** — It is the figure in our source material and we are
  confirming it against current Revenue Department rules. We are not giving tax advice from the
  stage. (See `factual-verification.md` §1.)
- **"Is this legal/tax advice?"** — No. The product makes a seller's own numbers visible; the
  rules it checks against are defined by us and are under professional review.
- **"What actually works today?"** — Reading documents, checking them against our rules, and
  showing the result. Everything else on the deck is labelled roadmap. (See
  `capabilities-vs-roadmap.md`.)
- **"Why will a seller pay?"** — They pay at the moment tax becomes real — a filing period, or
  approaching the threshold. That is why the model is free to start and priced per filing period
  or per year rather than monthly.

## Chapter 08 — driving the three screens

The screens advance by themselves every four seconds, so you can talk straight through it. If you
want to hold one — a judge leans in, or you are asked a question — **click that step in the list**
and the rotation stops for the rest of the session. The three dots under the laptop show where you
are. Say the roadmap line at the end, not the beginning; it lands as confidence, not as an excuse.

## Chapter 12 — the team

One line each, in this order: name, what you own, and why you are the person to own it. Do not read
the card aloud word for word; the card is for the judges' eyes while you talk. Then the closing
line, and stop.

**Before you present:** the three names are placeholders (*ชื่อ–นามสกุล*) and all three photographs are now in place. The name fix is a one-line edit each — see `content-map.md` chapter 12.

## The last line

End on the final line and stop. Do not add a summary after it.

> "So small businesses can focus on growing — not worrying."

Then: "ขอบคุณครับ."
